## What is Industrium?

Industrium is a Minecraft network built around a single large, hand-crafted world. Instead of a collection of disconnected minigames, you explore one persistent map with quests, creatures, powers and equipment that were designed specifically for it.

Our goal is a level of polish comparable to the biggest networks, with an identity of our own.

## Choose how you play

Both experiences connect to the same server, the same world and the same players. Only the way the game looks and sounds changes.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Vanilla Experience" icon="gamepad" href="/vanilla-experience">
    No installation. Join with a standard Minecraft Java client.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Immersive Experience" icon="sparkles" href="/immersive-experience">
    Custom 3D models, sounds, animations and menus through the Industrium launcher.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  New here? Start in vanilla to try the server, and switch to Immersive whenever you want. The modded client is free and entirely optional.
</Tip>

## What makes it different

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="One world, not a lobby of minigames" icon="globe">
    Everything in Industrium takes place in a single persistent world. Quests, creatures and equipment belong to that world and are designed to fit together.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Designed around a custom client" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    The Immersive client was built knowing what happens on the server side. Models, textures, sounds, animations and menus were all rebuilt to match the gameplay, not bolted on afterwards.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Travel without interruption" icon="bolt">
    Our infrastructure lets you cross between servers without a loading screen, so the world feels continuous.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Nobody is left behind" icon="users">
    The Immersive client never gates content. Players on vanilla and on Immersive share the same world.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
