<Note>
  The Immersive Experience is **free and optional**. Everything it adds is visual and audio. The server content is the same as in the [Vanilla Experience](/vanilla-experience).
</Note>

## What is it?

The Immersive Experience is the most complete way to play Industrium. A modded client, delivered through the **Industrium launcher**, replaces Minecraft's look and feel with our own: models, textures, sounds, animations and menus are all rebuilt, and designed around what happens on the server.

No resource packs to hunt down, no mods to assemble by hand. The launcher installs and updates everything for you.

<Info>
  You can play alongside friends who use the vanilla version. You share the same world, just with a different look.
</Info>

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the launcher">
    Get the Industrium launcher from the official website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Immersive Experience">
    The launcher downloads and configures the modded client automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Start the game and join Industrium directly from the launcher.
  </Step>
</Steps>
